OBS Stream Dock

Set your stream title and category on Twitch and YouTube at once, right from a dock inside OBS Studio.

What it does

  • Adds a dock to OBS with fields for title, Twitch category and an optional YouTube description.
  • One click on Apply updates Twitch and YouTube and shows the result for each platform.
  • If your YouTube broadcast doesn't exist yet, the title is kept and applied as soon as you go live.
  • Everything runs locally inside OBS. There is no server operated by the author and no account to create.

How it works

An OBS Python script starts a small web server on 127.0.0.1 while OBS is running. The dock is a Custom Browser Dock pointing to it. You log in once to Twitch and Google with a device code; the plugin then talks directly from your PC to the Twitch and YouTube APIs.
