Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

OBS Stream Info ("the plugin") is an open-source script for OBS Studio. It runs entirely on your own computer. The author does not operate any server for it and does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

What the plugin accesses

When you log in, you grant the plugin access to your accounts through the official login pages of Twitch and Google:

  • Twitch (scope channel:manage:broadcast): your user name and ID, your channel's current title and category, and the permission to change title and category. It also searches Twitch categories when you type in the category field.
  • YouTube (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): your channel name and ID, your live broadcasts (title, description, status, scheduled time), and the permission to change the title and description of these broadcasts.

The plugin only uses this access to show and update your stream title, category and description when you click Apply, and to apply a pending title to your YouTube broadcast once it is live. It does not read or change anything else.

Where your data is stored

  • Login tokens and a pending YouTube title are stored in plain files in the plugin's data folder on your computer.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere except directly from your computer to the Twitch and Google/YouTube APIs.
  • The plugin contains no analytics, tracking or telemetry.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. It never leaves your computer except for the API requests to Twitch and Google described above.

Google API Services

The plugin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed via the YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

  • Click log out in the dock. This revokes the token and deletes the local token file.
  • You can also revoke access at any time in your Google account settings and in your Twitch connections.
  • Deleting the plugin folder removes all data it has stored.
